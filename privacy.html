This notice explains how Catacombs uses email addresses submitted through the event and loyalty updates form.

What we collect and why

When you opt in, we collect your email address and the time you gave consent so the Catacombs team can manage event announcements and occasional loyalty updates. The website does not send emails automatically; signups can be reviewed and exported by an administrator.

Storage and access

Signup records are stored in the Catacombs Supabase database. Access is limited to the private website administration service. We do not ask for your name through this form. We keep your address while you are subscribed and remove it when you withdraw consent or request deletion.

Your choices

You can withdraw consent, request access or correction, or ask us to remove your address by emailing sam@catacombsevents.com. We will remove it from the active signup list. If an email delivery provider is connected later, this notice should be updated to identify that provider before campaigns are sent.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data? Contact sam@catacombsevents.com.
